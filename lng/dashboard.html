How close were past estimates?

The method was selected using earlier months, then checked on these 12 later months. The range was fixed using a separate earlier period.

Timing limitation: these tests estimate each month using the preceding month’s reported exports. They do not reproduce the publication delay or earlier versions of the DOE data. This is a retrospective planning benchmark.

Understand the data and download the numbers

One terminal, one primary source: the U.S. Department of Energy LNG export workbook. Terminal activity includes reported allocations by different exporters; it does not represent Venture Global’s consolidated sales or revenue.

A shipment may appear in multiple allocation rows. We add all reported volumes and count distinct vessel/date combinations as estimated departures. Vessel naming differences can affect this count.

Review flags mark months more than 25% below the preceding six-month average. They are prompts for investigation, not proof of a shutdown, failure, or safety issue.
