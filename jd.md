# Senior Data Scientist - Venture Global LNG

## About the Job

Venture Global LNG (“Venture Global”) is a long-term, low-cost provider of American-produced liquefied natural gas. The company’s Louisiana-based export projects service the global demand for North American natural gas and support the long-term development of clean and reliable North American energy supplies. Using reliable, proven technology in an innovative plant design configuration, Venture Global’s modular, mid-scale plant design delivers the same efficiency and operational reliability as traditional designs at significantly lower capital cost.

---

## Position

**Title:** Senior Data Scientist  
**Location:** Arlington, VA

---

## Summary

The Senior Data Scientist is responsible for developing and maintaining data analytics solutions and machine learning algorithms. As a senior role, expectations include:

- Mentoring junior data scientists
- Further developing the data science lifecycle and best practices
- Contributing to strategic discussions

**Ideal candidates excel in:**  
Communication, technical proficiency, collaboration, mentorship, strategic thinking, and continuous learning.

*This position is based in Arlington, VA and reports to the Director of Business Intelligence within IT, under the Vice President of Applications.*

---

## Duties & Responsibilities

- Develop solutions to business problems using the data science lifecycle
- Develop and maintain data analytics solutions and machine learning algorithms
- Create comprehensive project plans for data science initiatives (including solution architecture, resourcing, dependencies)
- Use predictive modeling and classification to improve LNG production efficiency and mitigate safety/downtime risks
- Mine and analyze data from data lakes/warehouses to drive optimization
- Collaborate with stakeholders to identify opportunities for business solutions using data science
- Identify key decision points and communicate solution options to stakeholders
- Provide ETL requirements to data engineers for data analytics
- Document data science algorithms for maintainability
- Work with IT leadership to define/enhance the data science lifecycle, tech stack, and architecture
- Design data architecture for future platform growth (data warehousing, ML, streaming analytics, data visualization)
- Assist with testing, governance, data quality, training, and documentation efforts
- Mentor junior data scientists and data analysts
- Engage in stakeholder workshops to translate business requirements into technical solutions
